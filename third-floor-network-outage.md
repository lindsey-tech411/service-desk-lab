# Third-Floor Network Outage

**Service Desk Simulator | INC0012858 | Critical | Resolved**

## Issue and impact

Employees and conference rooms on the third floor lost internet access.
The second floor still had connectivity. The outage affected multiple
departments and blocked the customer service team from accessing its CRM.

## Troubleshooting and resolution

Because the reported outage was limited to one floor, I checked that
floor's network equipment in the simulator's Server Room. I found the
Floor 3 Switch offline and rebooted it. Connectivity returned, and the
simulator marked the ticket resolved.

## What I learned

Checking who is affected helps narrow down which equipment to investigate.
Before closing an outage ticket, I would confirm with an affected user
that they can reach the application they need for work, such as the CRM.
