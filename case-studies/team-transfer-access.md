# Team Transfer Access Update

**Environment** — Service Desk Simulator  
**Ticket** — INC0012860  
**Status** — Resolved

## Request

An employee transferred from Engineering to IT Infrastructure.
The request called for granting access to infrastructure tools
and revoking access tied to the employee's previous team.

## What I did

I opened the employee's account in the simulator's Directory,
removed the employee from the `Engineering` group, and granted
access for the new IT Infrastructure role. I also updated the
role and department fields.

## Outcome

The simulator marked the ticket as Resolved after I removed
the previous team's access and granted access for the new role.

## What I learned

For a team transfer, I would verify both sides of the change
before closing the ticket: the account is in the IT Infrastructure
group and has been removed from the Engineering group. I would
also leave department and reporting-line changes to HR, as
specified in this request.
