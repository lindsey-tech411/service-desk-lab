# Customer Support PC Replacement

**Service Desk Simulator | INC0012871 | Critical | Resolved**

## Issue and impact

A Customer Support agent's desktop would not power on. The agent had
tested the wall outlet with another device and reported no lights, fan
activity, or startup beep from the desktop. The agent couldn't take calls
while calls were waiting in the queue.

## Resolution

I used the simulator's Computer Deployment tool to set up a replacement
desktop and sent it to the agent. The simulator marked the ticket resolved.

## What I learned

The simulator called for a server-imaged desktop because the agent relied
on a legacy softphone and on-premises device policies. The replacement
needed to support the agent's call workflow as well as power on.

For a similar incident, I would confirm the power cable with a known-good
replacement, verify that the agent could take calls on the new desktop,
and arrange to recover the failed unit.
